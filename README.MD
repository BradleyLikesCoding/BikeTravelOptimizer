# Suspension Fork Travel Optimizer
A device that constantly measures the amount of travel your bike is using during a ride, and helps you optimize your rebound, compression, and air pressure to get the most out of your suspension.
## How its gonna work:
- Linear potentiometer to measure travel
- Some sort of way to mount it very tightly to the fork
- An ESP32, RPI Zero 2w or something similar to store data
- The microcontroller creates a wifi network and if you join it you can go to a website and it will show you the statistics
